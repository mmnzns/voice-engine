# The v0.4.0 models on a Windows PC, 2026-11-16

Speed and test results for the four models added in v0.4.0, with the numbers behind the `speed` and `accuracy` dots in the
catalog. Measured by the examples and tests in this repository; no transcript, prompt or reply was printed or kept, only
counts, sizes, times and backend names.

## The machine

| | |
|---|---|
| Chip | AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D, 8 cores, 16 logical cores |
| Memory | 32 GB |
| System | Windows 11 Pro |
| Toolchain | rustc 1.98.1, `--release` for everything measured |
| Graphics | An RTX 5070 Ti is installed. Nothing here used it: every model ran on the processor (`backend` `cpu`) |

One model at a time; each example loads one model, measures it, drops it, and loads the next.

## Speech: one 29 s track, the processor

`cargo run --release -p voice-engine-stt --example bench_v040`. The track is a paragraph of different sentences spoken by
a Windows voice (Microsoft Zira, made at run time with System.Speech): a repeated clip makes Cohere and Whisper Turbo stop
after the first pass. Each line is one load, one warm-up decode, then 3 timed decodes; the median and the range of the 3.
`Timing::Segments`, no language (Cohere: English). Threads is the engine's rule (three quarters of the logical cores = 12).
Real-time factor = 29.0 s over the median decode.

| Model | Load | Decode median (range) | Real time | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whisper Large Turbo (compressed), for comparison | 0.68 s | 35.68 s (34.30 to 40.78) | 0.8x | 6 segments, 85 words |
| Parakeet Ultra, for comparison | 1.22 s | 0.47 s (0.46 to 0.48) | 62.3x | 7 segments, 88 words |
| Parakeet Unified (25 languages) | 3.85 s | 0.68 s (0.67 to 0.68) | 42.4x | 7 segments, 88 words |
| Cohere Transcribe | 1.46 s | 12.52 s (12.14 to 12.61) | 2.3x | 1 segment, 0 words (no word times), 456 characters |
| Multitalker Parakeet TDT (two voices talking over each other) | 5.55 s | 1.37 s (1.36 to 1.38) | 21.1x | 4 turns, 2 speakers, 167 words |

Whisper Turbo on the processor is slow on this PC (the Mac's processor ran it at 4.7x); it is here only as a comparison on
the same machine, and its catalog dots come from the Mac's measurements with the card. The Multitalker track is the same
paragraph (Zira) with a second voice (Microsoft David) saying other sentences from 2 s in, cut to 29 s; the speed depends
on the number of speakers, because the model runs once for each.

## Local AI: Gemma 4 E4B

`cargo run --release -p voice-engine-client --example bench_llm -- gemma-4-e4b granite-4.2-3b`, the processor (`gpu:
false`), context 4096, 50 words in, thinking off, temperature 0, 3 timed requests after 1 warm-up. The Qwen models are not on

| Model | Load | Prompt read | Reply median | Output tokens | Tokens per second |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gemma-4-e4b | 1.58 s | 0.97 s | 3.24 s | 40 | 17.4 |

## How the numbers became dots

The dots are 1 to 3, relative to the entries that were there. The calibration is the Mac table of `bench-2026-10-29.md`:
Parakeet Ultra at 60x and Whisper Base at 37x on the processor have 3 dots of speed; Whisper Turbo at 20x on Metal has 2;
the large Whisper models have 1. Against Parakeet Ultra measured on the same PC (62x), that is a speed of at least about half
of it for 3, from a third down to about a twelfth for 2, and below that for 1.

| Entry | Speed | Accuracy | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `parakeet-unified-25` | 3 | 2 | 42.4x is 0.68 of Parakeet Ultra's 62.3x. Accuracy: on NVIDIA's own test (the card of `nvidia/nemotron-labs-unified-asr-0.6b`, average of 44 runs over the 25 languages) its word error rate is 9.49 against Parakeet v3's 10.55, so it is above Parakeet v3 (1 dot) and level with Parakeet Ultra and the other Nemotron (2) |
| `cohere-transcribe` | 1 | 3 | 2.3x is 0.04 of Parakeet Ultra: below Whisper Turbo on the processor on the Mac (4.7x) and in the range of the large Whisper models, and there is no card path. Accuracy: its card gives an average English word error rate of 5.42 against 7.44 for Whisper Large v3 on the Open ASR leaderboard, so it has the dots Whisper Large v3 has |
| `multitalker-tdt` | 2 | 2 | 21.1x is 0.34 of Parakeet Ultra (Whisper Turbo on Metal, 2 dots, is 0.33 of it), for two speakers; it gets slower with more. Accuracy: on one speaker its card gives about the same word error rate as plain Parakeet TDT v3 (2.0 against 1.9 on LibriSpeechMix, 5.7 against 6.8 on MLS), and on overlapping speech 4.4 to 16.7 against 36.7 to 53.7 for the plain model, so it has the 2 of the models above Parakeet v3 |
| `gemma-4-e4b` | 3 | 2 | 17.4 tokens per second is 0.73 of Granite 4.2 3B's 23.8 (3 dots); Qwen3.5 9B (2 dots) is about half. Accuracy: the research found Gemma 4 E4B below Qwen3.5 4B on MMLU-Pro (69.4 against 79.1) and GPQA (58.6 against 76.2), and Qwen3.5 4B has 2, so Gemma may not have more; it has 2 |

The word error rates and the benchmark scores are the models' cards' own numbers, not measured here.

## What ran, and what passed

On this PC, in `--release`, with `--ignored --test-threads=1`:

| Test file | Tests | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `real_cohere.rs` (`cohere-transcribe`) | 8 | all pass |
| `real_multitalker.rs` (`multitalker-tdt` and `diarization`, two Windows voices) | 5 | all pass |
| `real_parakeet.rs` (`parakeet-ultra`, and the two new cases for `parakeet-unified-25`) | 12 | all pass |
| `crates/client/tests/real_gemma.rs` (`gemma-4-e4b` through the real helper) | 4 | all pass |

Facts the tests show:

* Cohere loads from a flat folder: ONNX Runtime finds `encoder_model_quantized.onnx_data` and `_data_1` by their relative
  names next to the `.onnx` file. Loading takes 1.4 s.
* Cohere sometimes writes a segment for silence (20 ms to 35 s of zeros: in one run of the test 3 of the 4 lengths gave a
  segment, in another all 4 did; its card says it is eager to transcribe). The engine adds no voice detection; the test
  checks only the shape of what comes back.
* Cohere's inverse text normalisation switch did not help: with punctuation on the model writes numbers as digits ("23")
  either way, and on one of three phrases switching it on turned the digits into words. The engine leaves it off, so
  numbers come as digits, as Whisper and Parakeet write them.
* Multitalker found 2 speakers in two voices one after the other, and in two voices talking over each other (turns that
  overlap for 11.6 s of a 15.7 s track), and a 74 s track of both came back with turns across all of it on one clock.
* The AI helper could not build a Gemma 4 prompt: llama.cpp's built-in template engine answers "error -1" for its
  template. The helper now writes that prompt itself (`crates/llm/src/gemma.rs`). With it, Gemma answers in a context of 4096 and in
  65536 tokens (`context_16gb`), with no thought-channel marks in the reply, gives JSON for a schema, and is unchanged by a
  thinking request (the helper's `inspect` says `thinks` false, as the catalog does).
